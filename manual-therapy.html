What It's Used For

When Manual Therapy Can Help

Joint Stiffness

Gentle joint mobilization to restore range of motion in stiff or restricted joints.

Frozen Shoulder

Progressive hands-on techniques to ease shoulder stiffness over time.

Back & Neck Pain

Manual mobilization and soft tissue release to ease muscular tension.

Post-Surgical Stiffness

Careful, graded mobilization to help restore movement after surgery.

Myofascial Tightness

Soft tissue techniques to release tight fascia and improve tissue mobility.

Sports Recovery

Hands-on work to support muscle recovery and movement quality between training sessions.

Our Approach
