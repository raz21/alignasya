Conditions We Treat

Common Sports Injuries We Manage

ACL & Ligament Injuries

Pre- and post-surgical rehab for ACL, MCL and PCL injuries, focused on restoring stability and confidence in movement.

Muscle Strains & Tears

Hamstring, quadriceps and calf strains managed through graded loading and progressive return-to-sport protocols.

Tendon Injuries

Tennis elbow, jumper's knee and Achilles tendinopathy addressed with targeted strengthening and load management.

Ankle Sprains

Balance, proprioception and strength work to prevent recurring sprains and instability.

Shoulder Instability

Rotator cuff and shoulder stabilization programs for throwing and racquet sport athletes.

Stress Fractures

Guided load progression and biomechanical assessment to support safe bone healing and a gradual return to training.

Our Approach

A Structured Path Back to Sport

Every athlete's recovery is different. Our sports injury programs are built around where your body actually is — not a generic timeline — moving through assessment, rebuilding strength, and reintroducing sport-specific movement before a graded return to full activity.

  • ✓ Detailed kinetic and movement assessment
  • ✓ Individualized strength & conditioning plans
  • ✓ Manual therapy, taping and electrotherapy as needed
  • ✓ Sport-specific movement retraining
  • ✓ Graded return-to-play protocols
